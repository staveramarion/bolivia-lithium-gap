Bolivia's Lithium Gap: Reserves vs. Reality

This analysis examines why Bolivia, holder of one of the world's largest lithium resourse bases has converted so little of it into actual exports, using the countries of Chile and Argentina as comparative benchmarks. The data covers the years 2017-2023, sourced from the UN Comtrade via World Bank WITS.
